# Design System: Cenarepas

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "El Fogón Artesanal"**

The landing opens on hands kneading corn dough by hand — the whole visual system radiates outward from that single image. Warmth over slickness: terracota and toasted-maize tones instead of corporate blue/gray, soft tinted shadows instead of neutral drop-shadows, frosted glass over the dark hero video instead of hard cards. Every section reads as "made by hand at a real factory," not "generated by an app template." Sections alternate between warm-light (crema/beige) and warm-dark (terracota gradient, slate) grounds, always keeping the terracota accent as punctuation rather than wallpaper.

Explicit anti-reference: this must not read as a generic delivery-app UI (Rappi/UberEats-style transactional cards, flat blue/red brand chips, stock food photography). The tone stays artisanal and editorial — closer to a family bakery's own site than a marketplace listing.

**The admin extension.** The internal Panel de Datos (`/admin`, "Operate" mode) is this identity's first extension beyond the landing: the same terracota/maíz/verde-hoja palette and tinted-shadow elevation carry over wholesale, but the job changes from persuading to scanning. Two things were added specifically for that job: Oswald (uppercase, condensed) for section labels/titles so dense screens still read hierarchy at a glance, and IBM Plex Mono for every numeric or tabular value so figures stay legible and tabular-aligned. The panel's own signature is a golden-angle kernel-spiral SVG cluster (`PedidosFlowHeader.jsx`) — two clusters of maize-kernel ellipses, placed with the botanical golden angle (137.50776°), animating in once at the top of the panel to represent the live pedido flow between Bello Oriente and Aranjuez. It is used exactly once, as the panel's opening signature, not repeated as decoration elsewhere.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm, food-and-craft palette (terracota / maíz dorado / verde hoja) on a crema base — never corporate blue/gray.
- Pill-shaped buttons and badges everywhere; softer rounded rectangles (not pills) for cards and form fields.
- Tinted, colored shadows (`shadow-brand/30`, `shadow-black/25`, and on the dashboard `rgba(61,42,23,…)` marrón-tinted shadows) instead of flat neutral shadows.
- Frosted glass (`bg-white/10 backdrop-blur-md`) used specifically where content sits on the dark hero video or a dark section — never on light sections.
- Scroll-reveal motion (fade + rise, `whileInView`/staggered mount) on every section; hover states lift and scale rather than just recolor.
- A serif italic accent (Playfair Display) marks the one emotional phrase per landing section; the dashboard instead uses Oswald uppercase for its section identity, and IBM Plex Mono for every number — everything else in both surfaces is Poppins.
- The maize-kernel golden-angle spiral cluster is CENAREPAS's own signature visual motif, native to the dashboard's opening viewport.

## Colors

Warm and food-derived: terracota clay as the anchor, toasted maize as the spark, leaf green as the one "natural/quality" signal — all sitting on a crema (not white) base so nothing feels sterile.

### Primary
- **Terracota de Horno** (`#c1502d`): the brand color. CTA buttons, active nav state, section headings on light backgrounds, primary icon fills. Used deliberately sparingly outside CTAs and headings — it is a punctuation color, not a fill color. On the dashboard: KPI icon tints, the Ventas chart line/fill, the protagonist card's top border, the "hot" (destacada) bar in Producción por Línea.
- **Terracota Profunda** (`#8a3418`): hover/pressed state for terracota surfaces, and the darker end of the `#ventajas` gradient. On the dashboard: section-title color (VENTAS/PRODUCCIÓN), destacada-line label color.
- **Terracota Suave** (`#e2895f`): lightest step of the brand ramp; the lighter end of the `#ventajas` gradient, occasional accent border.

### Secondary
- **Maíz Dorado** (`#e8b23d`): the "tradition/quality" accent — underline rules beneath section headings, checkmark bullets in Ventajas, stat icon tint. Never used as a large fill; always a small, high-contrast punctuation mark. On the dashboard: the kernel-cluster gradient's midtone, the pulse rings around each sede node, the Control y Monitoreo section's line-chart accent and feature-tile icon color.

### Tertiary
- **Verde Hoja de Mazorca** (`#5a7a3a`): quality/approval signal only (the "control de calidad aprobado" icon in About, the checkmark badges in Ventajas). Reserve it for trust/approval moments, not general decoration. On the dashboard: positive-trend pills, the Ventas KPI sparkline, the highest Producción bar tier.

### Neutral
- **Crema Masa** (`#fffbf0`): the landing's base background — replaces plain white everywhere except explicit white sections (About). On the dashboard: tooltip background, the Control y Monitoreo gradient's lightest stop.
- **Marrón Café Tostado** (`#3d2a17`): default body/foreground text on light backgrounds; also the base hue for every dashboard drop-shadow (`rgba(61,42,23,…)`).
- **Beige Harina** (`#f5ecd8`): muted surface (product-grid tint at 50–60% opacity, card interiors). On the dashboard: table header fill in Asistente de Reportes, one KPI icon chip.
- **Arena de Borde** (`#e8dcc0`): hairline borders on light sections — the dashboard's default card/table border throughout.
- **Horno Nocturno** (`#2a1206`): the hero's darkest base, behind the kneading video. On the dashboard: primary heading/value text color on light cards.
- **Reflejo de Maíz** (`#fbd28a`): the hero badge's pulsing dot and halo; on the dashboard, the kernel gradient's brightest stop and the Control y Monitoreo section-title color.
- **Masa Tibia** (`#fde8b8`): the hero's serif italic subtitle line, sitting on the dark video. On the dashboard: one KPI icon chip, and stat-highlight text color in Control y Monitoreo.
- **Noche de Contacto** (`#0f172a`) / **Pie Oscuro** (`#020617`): the two darkest grounds (Contact, Footer) — deliberately outside the terracota ramp so the funnel's final stretch feels calmer and more trustworthy than the warm marketing sections above it.
- **Taupe Cálido** (`#78633f`) and **Taupe Cálido Oscuro** (`#6b5636`): muted/secondary text on light or crema grounds across the dashboard (KPI labels, card captions, table sub-text, the panel's intro paragraph). Both replace the original design-handoff values `#a8916f`/`#8a7550`, which measured ~3.0:1 and ~4.4:1 contrast against white/crema — under the 4.5:1 WCAG AA floor PRODUCT.md commits to. Use `#78633f`/`#6b5636` only on light backgrounds.
- **Beige de Humo** (`#a8916f`) and **Beige de Humo Claro** (`#c9a97e`): muted text reserved for the dark Control y Monitoreo section only — kept from the original handoff because they already pass ~6:1 against that section's near-black gradient base. Do not reuse these on a light/crema ground; use the Taupe Cálido pair there instead.

### Named Rules
**The Ember Rule.** Terracota fills (not text/icons/borders) appear on ≤2 elements per section — a CTA button and, at most, one background gradient. If a third terracota fill shows up, demote it to outline or icon.

**The Tinted Shadow Rule.** Shadows are never neutral gray; they inherit the color of what casts them (`shadow-brand/30` under a terracota button, `shadow-black/25` for dark-on-dark depth, `shadow-black/10` for glass panels; on the dashboard, every shadow is `rgba(61,42,23,…)` — marrón-tinted). A gray `shadow-lg` on a colored surface is a bug, not a style choice.

**The Light/Dark Muted-Text Rule.** Muted/secondary text is a conditional pair, not one universal value: on light or crema grounds use Taupe Cálido (`#78633f`) or Taupe Cálido Oscuro (`#6b5636`); on the dark Control y Monitoreo ground use Beige de Humo (`#a8916f`) or Beige de Humo Claro (`#c9a97e`). Never carry a dark-ground muted color onto a light ground — that reintroduces the sub-AA contrast the light-ground swap exists to fix.

## Typography

**Display/Accent Font:** Playfair Display italic (with Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif fallback) — landing only.
**Body/UI Font:** Poppins (with system-ui, sans-serif fallback) — both surfaces.
**Section Font (dashboard):** Oswald (weights 500–700, uppercase) — Panel de Datos section labels and titles only.
**Numeric/Tabular Font (dashboard):** IBM Plex Mono (weights 400–600) — every number: KPI figures, chart tooltip values, table cells, dark-section stats.
**Unused/reserved:** Dancing Script is loaded and tokenized (`--font-script`) but not currently used by any component — do not introduce it without a deliberate decision; remove the import if it stays unused.

**Character:** A confident, slightly bold sans (Poppins) carries structure and UI; one italic serif line (Playfair Display) breaks in per landing section to carry the emotional beat ("amor y tradición," "El Secreto de la Receta"). The pairing reads as "family bakery letterhead," not "startup SaaS." On the dashboard, the emotional beat is replaced by a functional one: Oswald's condensed uppercase gives dense operational screens a scannable section rhythm, and IBM Plex Mono gives every number the tabular, monospaced discipline an operator needs to compare figures at a glance — both fonts are scoped to `.dashboard-panel` in `dashboard.css` (a local `--font-mono` override) so they never bleed into the rest of the admin shell's `font-mono` usage.

### Hierarchy
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 3rem)`, line-height 1.04): the landing hero's sans line and each section's `h2`. Sized down from a typical hero display size — this hero is intentionally compact and corner-anchored, not a full-bleed giant headline.
- **Display/Accent** (500, 1.06em of its parent, line-height 1.05, italic): the one serif line per landing section — always immediately after or inside a headline, never standalone.
- **Title** (700, 1.5rem–1.875rem): card and subsection titles (product names, "Envíanos un mensaje").
- **Body** (400, 1rem–1.125rem, line-height 1.6): paragraphs; kept under ~60ch via `max-w-md`/`max-w-2xl` containers. The dashboard's intro paragraph uses the same weight/line-height at 1rem, capped at 62ch.
- **Label** (600, 0.6–0.7rem, letter-spacing 0.14em, uppercase): landing eyebrow badges ("PEDIDOS ABIERTOS…", "¿QUIÉNES SOMOS?"), stat captions, nav labels.
- **Section Title** (Oswald, 700, `clamp(1.9rem, 3vw, 2.6rem)` for card titles up to `clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 3rem)` for the Control y Monitoreo heading, uppercase): dashboard-only. Names a card row or major section ("VENTAS", "PRODUCCIÓN", "CONTROL Y MONITOREO") — always immediately above its content, never a paragraph or button label.
- **Section Label** (Oswald, 600, 0.68–0.8rem, letter-spacing 0.1–0.14em, uppercase): dashboard-only. Smaller category tags above a KPI grid ("Panorama General") or inside the kernel-flow header ("Flujo de Pedidos en Tiempo Real", "BELLO ORIENTE").
- **Data Value** (IBM Plex Mono, 400–700, ranging from 0.68rem tooltip labels up to 2.6rem — in practice ~0.75rem table cells, 0.85rem tooltip/table values, 1.5rem dark-section stats, 1.9–2rem KPI/chart totals): dashboard-only. Every numeric or tabular figure, always `tabular-nums` where the value updates (KPI count-up).

### Named Rules
**The One Serif Line Rule.** Each landing section gets exactly one Playfair Display italic line. It is always the emotional payoff of a headline, never a paragraph, list, or button label.

**The Numbers-Are-Mono Rule.** On the dashboard, any value that is a count, a currency figure, a percentage, or a table/tooltip cell renders in IBM Plex Mono, never Poppins — labels and prose stay Poppins, numbers are always mono. This is what makes KPI figures and table columns feel tabular and comparable at a glance.

## Layout

Container rhythm on the landing is consistent: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8` for standard sections, with two deliberate exceptions — the Hero (`max-w-6xl`, content corner-anchored bottom-left rather than centered) and the CTA (`max-w-4xl`, tightly centered for a single focused ask).

Section vertical rhythm alternates by weight: `py-16 sm:py-24` for content-heavy sections (About, Products, Contact), a shorter `py-14 sm:py-16` for the Stats strip (a connective beat, not a destination), and `py-20 sm:py-24` for the two brand-gradient sections (Ventajas, CTA).

Sections alternate ground color to create a visible rhythm down the page: dark hero video → white (About) → crema tint (Stats) → crema tint (Products, same tone as Stats so the two read as one connected beat) → terracota gradient (Ventajas) → darkest slate (Contact) → terracota gradient (CTA) → darkest slate (Footer). Two-column `lg:grid-cols-2` pairs (text + visual) recur for About, Ventajas' top half, and Contact; repeated-item rows use a 4-column grid on desktop collapsing to 2 or 1 (Stats, Ventajas' process steps, Products).

**Dashboard layout.** The Panel de Datos sits inside the existing admin shell (Sidebar/Header), no outer max-width container of its own beyond the shell. Internally it follows a KPI-first, protagonist-and-sibling rhythm: a full-width kernel-flow header, then a 4-up KPI grid (`grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`, `gap-5`), then an asymmetric `lg:grid-cols-[1.6fr_1fr]` row pairing the protagonist Ventas chart with the flatter Producción chart, then an even `lg:grid-cols-2` row for Top Clientes / Alertas, then the full-width Asistente de Reportes panel, then the dark Control y Monitoreo section which breaks the shell's padding (`-mx-6`) and re-establishes its own wider `max-w-[1440px]` container — the one deliberate width exception, matching its role as a distinct system-admin sub-view rather than an operational card row. Section-to-section spacing is tighter than the landing's (`mb-8`–`mb-10` vs. `py-16`+) because this is a scan surface, not a scroll narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid: flat on light sections, tinted-shadow + frosted-glass on dark ones. Product and content cards on crema/white grounds use a hairline border (`border-slate-100`) plus a very soft shadow that only intensifies on hover (`hover:shadow-xl hover:shadow-brand/10`) — depth is a hover response, not a resting state. On dark grounds (Hero, Contact, Ventajas' feature panel), depth comes from translucency and blur (`bg-white/10 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/20`) layered over the photo/gradient, not from shadows.

**Dashboard elevation.** The same hybrid model extends here, with one addition: a two-tier card hierarchy. Sibling cards (KPIs, Producción, Top Clientes, Alertas, Asistente) rest nearly flat (`0 1px 2px rgba(61,42,23,0.04)`) and only gain a lifted, wider shadow on hover/tap (`0 20px 32px -18px rgba(61,42,23,0.28)`, spring `stiffness 360 / damping 28`). The row's protagonist card (Ventas) instead carries elevation at rest — a wider, softer resting shadow (`0 24px 48px -20px rgba(61,42,23,0.22)`) that deepens further on hover (`0 28px 52px -18px rgba(61,42,23,0.3)`) — so it reads as the row's lead item before any interaction happens.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **cta-glow** (`box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.25), 0 0 0 rgba(193,80,45,.3)` via `shadow-xl shadow-black/25`): primary buttons on dark/photo grounds.
- **brand-glow** (`shadow-lg shadow-brand/30`): primary buttons on light grounds — the shadow is tinted terracota, not gray.
- **card-rest / card-hover** (`shadow-sm` → `hover:shadow-xl hover:shadow-brand/10`): product and content cards.
- **glass-lift** (`shadow-2xl` on a `bg-white/5–10 backdrop-blur-md` panel): the Contact form panel and Ventajas feature panel.
- **dashboard-card-rest** (`0 1px 2px rgba(61,42,23,0.04)`): sibling dashboard cards at rest.
- **dashboard-card-hover** (`0 20px 32px -18px rgba(61,42,23,0.28)`): sibling dashboard cards on hover/tap.
- **dashboard-protagonist-rest** (`0 24px 48px -20px rgba(61,42,23,0.22)`): the Ventas card at rest.
- **dashboard-protagonist-hover** (`0 28px 52px -18px rgba(61,42,23,0.3)`): the Ventas card on hover/tap.
- **dashboard-tooltip** (`0 14px 28px -6px rgba(61,42,23,0.32)`): chart tooltips (`CustomTooltip.jsx`).

### Named Rules
**The Glass-on-Dark-Only Rule.** Frosted glass (`bg-white/5–10` + `backdrop-blur`) is reserved for surfaces sitting on the hero video, a photo, or a dark/gradient section. Never apply it over the crema or white grounds — there it just looks like low contrast, not material.

**The Protagonist Card Rule.** In any dashboard row of siblings, at most one card is the protagonist: it gets a wider resting shadow, extra top/bottom padding (`pt-7 pb-5` vs. siblings' `pt-6 pb-4`), and a `3px` solid terracota top border (`border-t-[3px] border-t-[#c1502d]`). Every other card in the row stays flat at rest and only lifts on hover. Reserve the protagonist treatment for the row's primary metric (currently: Ventas) — giving two cards in the same row the top border defeats its purpose as a hierarchy signal.

## Shapes

Two registers, used deliberately: **pill** (`rounded-full`) for anything you press — primary/secondary buttons, badges, avatar/icon circles — and **soft rectangle** for anything you read or contain — cards at `1rem`–`1.5rem` radius, form fields and the contact/feature panels at `0.75rem`–`2.5rem`. Nothing in the system uses a sharp (0px) corner or a heavy visible border as its primary boundary; edges are established by background-color and shadow, with hairline borders (`border-white/10`, `border-slate-100`, dashboard's `#e8dcc0`) as a secondary reinforcement only.

The dashboard adds one intermediate radius step, `1.25rem`, for its densest card grid (KPIs, Top Clientes, Alertas) — between the landing's `1rem` inline-card radius and its `1.5rem` product-card radius — because these cards are smaller and a full `1.5rem` reads oversized at that size. Larger dashboard cards (Ventas, Producción, Asistente, the kernel-flow header) use the existing `1.5rem`/`rounded-3xl` step, so no new large-radius token was needed.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px) — no exceptions for primary/secondary CTAs.
- **Primary:** terracota fill, white text, asymmetric padding (`py-1.5 pr-1.5 pl-5`) that leaves room for a circular icon chip on the trailing edge; `shadow-lg shadow-brand/30` (light grounds) or `shadow-xl shadow-black/25` (dark/photo grounds).
- **Hover / Focus:** `hover:bg-brand-dark`, spring `whileHover={{ scale: 1.035, y: -2 }}` / `whileTap={{ scale: 0.975 }}` (stiffness ~420, damping ~26); focus ring is a 2px white ring offset from the section's own background color.
- **Outline/Ghost (secondary):** `border border-white/30 bg-white/10 backdrop-blur-md` on dark grounds, or a plain bordered pill on light grounds; same hover lift as primary, never gains a fill on hover (stays translucent). The dashboard's small action pills (Alertas' "Resolver"-style buttons, Asistente's send button) follow the same pill shape at a smaller scale (`h-8 w-8` icon pill, or `px-3 py-1.5` text pill), terracota text/fill against a white or crema chip.

### Badges / Eyebrows
- **Style:** pill, `bg-white/10 border border-white/25` on dark grounds or `bg-brand/10 text-brand` on light grounds; uppercase label type, a small dot (often animated with a pulse+halo) leading the text.
- **State:** static; the only animated badge is the Hero's "pedidos abiertos" dot (`pulse` + expanding `halo` keyframes).
- **Dashboard scope.** This eyebrow-badge pattern is a landing-only convention; the dashboard's `h1` intentionally carries no eyebrow/kicker element above it (see Do's and Don'ts).

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** `1.5rem` for product cards (`rounded-3xl`), `1rem` for inline content cards, up to `2.5rem` for the Contact form and Ventajas feature panel. Dashboard: `1.25rem` for KPI/Top Clientes/Alertas cards, `1.5rem` for Ventas/Producción/Asistente cards and the kernel-flow header.
- **Background:** white or crema on light sections; `bg-white/5–10` glass on dark sections. The dashboard's dark Control y Monitoreo section instead uses flat translucent tiles (`bg-white/[0.04]`, hairline `border-white/[0.08]`) over its own gradient ground, not full frosted blur — a lighter-weight variant of the glass treatment, appropriate to a data-dense sub-view rather than a hero/contact moment.
- **Shadow Strategy:** see Elevation & Depth — resting flat, shadow appears on hover for light-ground cards; the dashboard's protagonist card is the one exception that carries shadow at rest (The Protagonist Card Rule).
- **Border:** hairline only (`border-slate-100` light, `border-white/10–20` dark, `#e8dcc0` on the dashboard).
- **Internal Padding:** `p-5`–`p-8` depending on card size; product cards use `p-6` for the text block below the image. Dashboard cards: `p-5 sm:p-6` (KPI), `p-7` (Top Clientes, Alertas), `px-6 pt-6/7 pb-4/5` (Ventas, Producción), `p-6 sm:p-8` (Asistente).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `rounded-xl`, `bg-white/5 border border-white/10`, white text on the dark Contact section (no light-mode input exists yet in the codebase). The dashboard's Asistente de Reportes input follows the same `rounded-xl` register but on a light chip (`border border-[#e8dcc0] bg-[#fffbf0]`), since it sits on a white card, not a dark ground.
- **Focus:** border shifts to brand terracota (`focus:border-brand`) plus a subtle `whileFocus={{ scale: 1.01 }}` micro-scale — no glow/ring. Dashboard buttons/inputs use `focus-visible:outline focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-[#c1502d]` for keyboard focus.
- **Error / Disabled:** not yet implemented in code; when added, follow the Tinted Shadow Rule (a red-tinted, not gray, error state) and keep the same `rounded-xl` shape.

### Navigation
- **Style:** fixed header, transparent over the hero and filled (`bg-background/80 backdrop-blur-md`) once scrolled; nav links are Poppins semibold with an underline-style active/hover treatment (`landing-nav-link`); mobile collapses into a slide-down panel, not an overlay drawer. The dashboard lives inside the existing admin Sidebar/Header shell and does not add its own navigation chrome.

### Kernel-Spiral Signature (dashboard)
`PedidosFlowHeader.jsx`'s opening panel: two clusters of maize-kernel ellipses (22 per side) positioned with the golden angle (137.50776° per step, radius `6.4·√i`), filled with a radial gradient from `#fbd28a` through `#e8b23d` to `#a8471f`, animating in from a scattered burst to their resting spiral on mount (skipped/instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`). A single connecting arc pulses between the two clusters (Bello Oriente ↔ Aranjuez) with an expanding-ring halo, echoing the landing hero's pulsing eyebrow dot but reimagined as CENAREPAS's own botanical motif — the maize kernel — rather than a generic dot or icon. Used exactly once, at the top of the panel; not a decorative pattern to sprinkle elsewhere.

### KPI Card (dashboard)
Label + icon chip up top, a large IBM Plex Mono value with `tabular-nums` and a count-up animation, a trend pill (green `#5a7a3a`/terracota-toned `#a8471f` background at 10% opacity, arrow icon, signed percentage), and a small inline SVG sparkline unique per metric (area, dot-timeline, or bar), all inside a `1.25rem`-radius white card. Four KPIs per row; each staggers in with a `60ms`-per-index count-up delay after mount.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep terracota fills to ≤2 per section (The Ember Rule) — lean on the outline/ghost button or the gold accent instead of a second solid terracota block.
- **Do** use tinted shadows that match their surface's hue (brand/30, black/25, black/10, or the dashboard's `rgba(61,42,23,…)`) — never a plain gray `shadow-lg`.
- **Do** reserve frosted glass for dark/photo grounds only (The Glass-on-Dark-Only Rule).
- **Do** pair every serif italic accent line with a Poppins headline right above or around it — it never stands alone (The One Serif Line Rule).
- **Do** give every section a `whileInView`/staggered fade+rise entrance and every interactive card/button a spring hover lift, matching the existing timing (duration ~0.4–0.9s, stiffness ~250–420).
- **Do** render every dashboard number in IBM Plex Mono (The Numbers-Are-Mono Rule) and scope Oswald/IBM Plex Mono to `.dashboard-panel` — never let them leak into the rest of the admin shell's type.
- **Do** use Taupe Cálido (`#78633f`/`#6b5636`) for muted text on light dashboard grounds, and Beige de Humo (`#a8916f`/`#c9a97e`) only on the dark Control y Monitoreo ground (The Light/Dark Muted-Text Rule).
- **Do** limit the protagonist-card treatment (extra shadow, padding, terracota top border) to one card per row (The Protagonist Card Rule).

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce corporate blue/gray or flat neutral-gray shadows — that reads as the shadcn/ui admin theme leaking into the landing, not this system.
- **Don't** let the landing drift toward a generic delivery-app look (flat brand-color chips, stock food photography, dense transactional cards) — the explicit anti-reference for this system.
- **Don't** use a sharp 0px corner or a heavy solid border as a shape's primary boundary; corners stay soft (pill or ≥0.75rem radius) and borders stay hairline.
- **Don't** add Dancing Script to a component without a deliberate decision — it is loaded but currently unused across the codebase.
- **Don't** present the Contact section's phone/email/address values as real — see PRODUCT.md's Evidence on Hand; they are placeholders pending the client's real data.
- **Don't** add an eyebrow/kicker badge above the dashboard's `h1`. An original handoff pinned one there; the shipped build removed it during finish review as a craft-floor violation, and that removal held through ship. This is scoped to the dashboard only — the landing's own eyebrow badges (see Badges / Eyebrows) are a distinct, legitimate pattern for that surface and are not affected.
- **Don't** carry a dark-ground muted color (`#a8916f`, `#c9a97e`) onto a light/crema dashboard card, or a light-ground one onto the dark Control y Monitoreo section — the pair is conditional on ground color, not interchangeable (The Light/Dark Muted-Text Rule).
